One Second is the companion app for participants of motorsport events (regularity rallies and touring routes) organised with One Second (onesecond.es). It requires an entry in an event: each rider gets a personal address (QR) from the organisers.

HOW TO TEST (test rider created for App Review)
1. Open the app and tap "One Second Legends".
2. Tap VINCULAR (Link) > paste this test rider address and tap GUARDAR (Save):
   https://onesecond.es/legends/api/p/pjroqr4xu3fy
   (rider no. 99 "App Review (prueba)" in the test event of One Second Legends)
3. Tap EMITIR (Send) and allow location. The app starts timing the rider and sends the GPS position to the organisers' server every 20 seconds.
4. Lock the iPhone or switch to another app: the blue location indicator stays on and the positions keep arriving (the counter of sent positions keeps increasing when you come back). Tap PARAR (Stop) to end it.
5. AVISOS (Alerts) sends an incident alert with the position to the organisers; MENSAJES shows messages from the organisers.
6. One Second Navigator (main screen): choose a route and tap EMPEZAR to follow it on the map with voice guidance, also with the screen off.

WHY PERSISTENT BACKGROUND LOCATION (Guideline 2.5.4)
- A regularity rally is timed by the phone itself: the rider must pass hidden checkpoints at an exact average speed, and the app records the exact time (to a tenth of a second) when the GPS position crosses each checkpoint. Riders are on a motorcycle or in a classic car for several hours with the phone in a mount or in a pocket, usually with the screen locked. If location stopped in the background, the passages would be lost and the rider could not be classified.
- Safety: during the event the organisers follow every rider on a private map and can locate a rider who reports a fall or an accident.
- Turn-by-turn route guidance (One Second Navigator) also needs continuous location with the screen off.
- Significant-change or region monitoring are not precise enough (checkpoints are secret and timed to a tenth of a second).
- Location is only used between EMITIR and PARAR (or while navigating a route). The blue indicator is always shown (showsBackgroundLocationIndicator). Nothing is collected at any other time and it is never shared with third parties.

OTHER PLATFORMS (Guideline 2.3.10)
Build 24 removes every reference to other platforms from the app. The web pages of onesecond.es shown inside the app (event page, account) no longer mention them on iPhone and iPad.

ACCOUNT (OPTIONAL)
The app works without an account. Riders can optionally sign in with their onesecond.es account (email and password, Sign in with Apple or Google) to see their entries and results. Accounts can be deleted from "Mi cuenta".
