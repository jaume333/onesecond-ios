One Second is the companion app for participants of motorsport events (regularity rallies and touring routes) organised with One Second (onesecond.es). It requires an entry in an event: each rider gets a personal address (QR) from the organisers.

HOW TO TEST
1. Open the app and tap "One Second Rally".
2. Tap VINCULAR (Link) > paste this test rider address and tap GUARDAR (Save):
   https://onesecond.es/rally/api/p/v3sc5wq3bchs
3. Tap EMITIR (Send) and allow location "While Using the App". The app sends the GPS position to the organisers' server until you tap PARAR (Stop).
4. AVISOS (Alerts) sends an incident alert to the organisers; MENSAJES shows messages from the organisers.

BACKGROUND LOCATION
Location is only used while the rider has tapped EMITIR during an event (the blue location indicator is shown). It is needed so that the organisers can time the rider's passage through hidden checkpoints and locate them if they report an incident or accident. Location is never used when not sending and is never shared with third parties.
